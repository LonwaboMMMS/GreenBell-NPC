WHO WE ARE

A garden can grow

We believe that a garden can be much more than a place where vegetables grow.

It can provide nutritious food to a household, create a practical learning environment for young people, strengthen a school community and bring people together around a shared purpose.

Through our work, we are building practical, community-based solutions that help people become more involved in producing the food they eat.

01 — COMMUNITY AGRICULTURE

Community
Pilot Gardens

We establish and develop community-based pilot gardens where sustainable farming practices can be demonstrated and improved.

These gardens serve as practical learning spaces where we experiment with organic production methods, soil improvement, vermitechnology, crop production and efficient use of available resources.

The produce generated from these gardens contributes towards our community gifting programme, while produce that is sold helps generate income that can be reinvested into garden operations and future community activities.
